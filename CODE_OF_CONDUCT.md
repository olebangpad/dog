# Code of conduct

PixSpell follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, critique work and not people, and keep the space welcoming to people of every
background and experience level. Harassment of any kind isn't tolerated.

Report a problem privately to the maintainer (olebangpad on GitHub, through the contact details on their profile).
Reports are handled confidentially.
