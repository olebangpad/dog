Your login picks the look. Your top language picks the class and colors, your repos and stars the level, and today's activity what your hero is doing. Put it on your profile README and it's redrawn every day.

Put it on your profile

  1. In your profile repository (github.com/you/you), add this file as .github/workflows/pixspell-hero.yml. It draws your hero once a day and commits it.
    pixspell-hero.yml

How your hero is made

      

        
body, hat, off-handpicked from your login, so your hero always looks like you

        
class, weapon, colorsyour most used language across your own public repos: TypeScript makes a knight, Go a ranger,
          Rust a blacksmith, Python a druid, JavaScript a wizard ( languages have a class; the rest get one by name)

        
levelrepos, stars, followers and account age; the weapon upgrades at level 15 and 35

        
todayin battle if you were active in the last day, on a quest in the last week, otherwise resting at the inn

      

      
Only public data is read, from GitHub's API, in your browser. Everything you see is drawn by PixSpell, the same
        spell.char(), spell.action() and spell.walk() calls you can use in your own game.
        The card is CC0.
